# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want IndiDraw to be a welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, caste, religion, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, nationality, language, personal appearance, race or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Welcome newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Accept feedback gracefully and give credit for others' work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory language.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers using GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, issue or pull request, or by opening a private report from the repository's **Security** tab and marking it "Code of conduct". Reports are kept confidential.

## Enforcement

Maintainers will review reports promptly and fairly and may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation, for behaviour that violates this code. This applies in all project spaces and when representing the project in public.
